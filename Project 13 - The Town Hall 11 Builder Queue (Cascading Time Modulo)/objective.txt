● The Task: Ask the user for their current Gold storage integer, and then ask for the raw upgrade time in pure minutes (e.g., 15430 minutes).


● The Math: You must use cascading division (/) and modulo (%) operators to convert that massive integer of minutes into exact Days, Hours, and Minutes.

● The Trap: The Gem calculation must round up. If they are short 10,001 Gold, they need 2 Gems, not 1. C integer division automatically rounds down, so you have to engineer a mathematical workaround using modulo to detect remainders and add an extra Gem if necessary.
